# Stall icons and accessories

Nine transparent PNG assets generated with built-in imagegen. Full prompts and source provenance are in MANIFEST.json. Original source pixels are preserved; RenovationArt uses an alpha-bounds AtlasTexture for tight previews and hotspots.

Market accessories: hibiscus, rattan_lamp, pink_batik. Institutional accessories: office_plant, modern_pendant, steel_organizer. Action icons: renovate, style, supplies. Existing resource star art is reused for the renovation wallet.

Purchase costs remain 2/3/2 stars for plant/light/counter accessories; no coins are used. My Stalls previews, equips and removes decor, saving purchases immediately. School and Government Office Canteen only offer compatible institutional equipment; other locations offer market decor. Existing incompatible equipped records remain preserved but are not rendered.
